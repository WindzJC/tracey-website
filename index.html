THE BOOK

More than a book.
A way forward.

What if leadership wasn't about a title, but about the life you're choosing to create?

CEO of Your Life: Becoming the Leader Within is an interactive journal that invites you to take a closer look at your experiences, values and goals—and begin turning those insights into intentional action.

See how the journey works ↗

INSIDE THE JOURNEY

Read it. Reflect on it.
Make it yours.

Not a book of advice to passively absorb. A guided experience built around personal reflection, practical activities, and an action plan you develop along the way.

01 / REFLECT↗

Start with self-awareness.

Guided reflection questions encourage you to examine your experiences, choices, and the direction you want to take.

02 / DEFINE↗

Clarify what matters.

Thoughtful activities help you identify personal values, focus your goals, and consider what purposeful leadership means in your own life.

03 / ACT↗

Make a plan to move forward.

Action-planning exercises are designed to bring your reflections together into a personal leadership roadmap.

These features are summarized from Tracey's public description of the book. Actual interior pages will be shown only with her approval.

MEET THE AUTHOR

Tracey Young, MEd

Author · Educator · Advocate for intentional leadership

Tracey Young brings her experience in education and her passion for personal and professional growth to CEO of Your Life. Her message is grounded in a simple idea: everyone has the capacity to lead their own life with greater awareness, clarity and purpose.

Through the journal and conversations in her community, Tracey invites readers to move beyond inspiration and take meaningful steps toward the future they envision.
